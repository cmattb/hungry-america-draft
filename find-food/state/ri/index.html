  9. Rhody Outpost - University of Rhode Island Food Pantry

    Student Food Pantry, Urban Agriculture

    Address
    10 Tootell Road, Kingston, RI 02881
    Phone
    401-874-5633
    Hours
    Mon & Wed 3:00pm-5:00pm (during the academic year), Wed 1:00pm-3:00pm (during the summer).
    Who can get food
    Must be a student at the University of Rhode Island.
    Accessibility
    Rhody Outpost is wheelchair accessible via ramp. Volunteer can assist with carrying or loading items into the car. An accessible shuttle is available to transport students to the pantry. Additionally, if a student is not able to come and shop during open hours, they may use the mobile ordering system or ask a friend to pick up their items. Please email or call ahead to ask about delivery options - only available in select cases.

    Website · Directions

    More about this program

    "Fresh food (vegetables, fruit, milk, eggs, yogurt, bread), Non-perishable items, Household/personal care items (toilet paper, menstrual products, soap, etc.). We offer household goods, such as dishes, small appliances, books, decor, etc., as they are donated to us. We collaborate with our Gender and Sexuality Center and Health Services to offer a Care Closet with gender affirming clothing and supplies, open to all. Other resources include referrals to our SNAP outreach office and other food rescue organizations, and volunteer opportunities for students."
