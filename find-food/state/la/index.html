    Our Daily Bread is a local food bank that has faithfully served the disadvantaged of Tangipahoa Parish since 1999. ​Our Daily Bread Food Bank in Hammond, Louisiana, collaborates with several community organizations to address hunger and provide essential services. Notable partnerships include:​ Hand of Hope: Operates a hot meal site that distributes thousands of nutritious meals monthly, with meal distributions every Tuesday, Thursday, and the first and third Saturdays. ​ Helping Hands Ministries: Through this partnership, clients receive hot meals alongside their food boxes during the 1st and 3rd Saturday outreaches. ​ Joyce Meyer Ministries – Hand of Hope: Assisted in establishing a kitchen to support meal services at the food bank. ​ Council on Aging: Provides food, transportation, and other assistance for seniors. ​ Northlake Homeless Coalition: Offers housing services to those in need. ​ Southeast Advocates for Family Empowerment (SAFE): Provides services for individuals fleeing domestic violence. ​ Quad Area Community Action Agency: Offers programs for low-income families, including energy assistance, home weatherization, veteran services, youth education, job training, emergency assistance, and Medicaid applications. Their mission is to to alleviate hunger and make an eternal impact by sharing kindness and compassion with everyone who is in need.
