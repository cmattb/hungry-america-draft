  4. Digimart - Lutheran Social Services of Nevada

    Food Pantry

    Address
    4323 Boulder Highway, Las Vegas NV 89121
    Phone
    702-639-1730
    Hours
    Mon - Thurs 9:00am to 1:00pm
    Who can get food
    Please bring identification for each family member, social security details, proof of income, and proof of address if you can. First-time visitors shoouldsign up in person; returning clients can log in to DigiMart on the website to continue managing their grocery needs effeciency.
