    "The Gallatin County Food Pantry is here as a resource to those who need assistance through a difficult time for many reasons. We serve the unemployed as well as the underemployed, households whose income does not stretch to cover food expenses, older adults on fixed incomes, temporarily homeless households and many others who are suffering due to unfavorable economic conditions. It is our hope that this assistance will ease some of the financial hardships so many are facing and that we will be a bridge of support to alleviate the fear of hunger in our community."
