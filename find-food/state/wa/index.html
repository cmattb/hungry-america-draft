    Food Bank

    Who can get food

    Accessibility
    Not accessible. Home Delivery service is available for people with disabilities, as well as curbside grocery pick-up.

    Directions

    More about this program

    The Vashon Food Bank collaborates with several community organizations to enhance food security and provide comprehensive services to Vashon Island residents. Northwest Harvest is a statewide hunger relief agency that supplies food to the Vashon Food Bank, ensuring a steady source of nutritious food for those in need. Food Lifeline is another key supplier of food, working to address hunger in Western Washington by providing food to various food banks, including Vashon Food Bank. Vashon Island Growers Association (VIGA) Food Access Partnership collaborates with the food bank to distribute Farm Bucks, which enable access to locally grown produce, supporting both food security and local farmers. Vashon Youth & Family Services (VYFS) partners with the food bank to assist families and individuals in navigating social services, ensuring they receive the resources they need beyond food assistance. Vashon Island Pet Protectors (VIPP) works with the food bank to provide supplemental pet food to community members, ensuring that pets in need also receive proper nourishment.

    FB: @Vashon Maury Community Food Bank, Instagram: @vashoncommunityfoodbank
