    "Neighbors Serving Neighbors was founded in response to Covid-19 Pandemic and Winter Storm Uri in 2021 by Stephanie Myers. Stephanie and a small team started baking and donating breads to folks in need, collecting donations to help folks pay utility bills, and purchasing hot water heaters for those whose needed replacement. A small table outside her front porch for can donations quickly grew into the pantry today that serves over 7,000 visits from families in Bartlett and the surrounding areas."
