    Food Bank

    Address
    3890 West State St., Boise, ID, 83703
    Phone
    208-345-2734
    Hours
    Mon, Wed and Fri: 11:00am to 2:00pm
    Who can get food
    Our services are limited to those who reside in the 83702, 83703, 83706 and 83714 area codes, as well as veterans and active military living anywhere in the treasure valley. New clients should arrive no later than 30 min before closing to complete paper work. You must provide: Photo ID for all household members over 18. Proof of address dated withing 60 days for everone over age 18, ID for all household members under age 18. Total household income. Names and dob of all individuals in household.
    Accessibility
    Drive-Thru Service. We have been experiencing overcrowding and near accidents in our parkin lot. If the lot is full, please park elsewhere and return in 15 minutes. Building is closed to clients. Drive-Thru only

    Website · Directions

    More about this program

    St. Mary's Food Bank in Boise provides food assistance to residents of specific zip codes and veterans/active military in the Treasure Valley. St. Mary's Food Bank in Boise, Idaho, collaborates with several community partners to support its mission of providing food assistance to those in need. Notable partners include the Idaho Diaper Bank, Idaho Community Foundation, and Idaho Power Employee Community Fund. Additionally, organizations such as the Boise Southwest Rotary Foundation, St. Mary's Knights of Columbus, Idaho Food Bank, Idaho Community Fund, and Albertsons have made financial contributions to support the food bank's initiatives. Their services operate on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday from 11 AM to 2 PM. Clients remain in their cars while volunteers deliver food to them.
