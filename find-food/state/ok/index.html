  6. Lawton Food Bank

    Food Bank

    Address

    Phone
    580-353-7994
    Hours
    TEFAP (Regular Distribution) Mon and Wed: 10:00am-1:00pm, Tues and Thurs: 1:00pm-5:30pm, CSFP: (Senior Box Distribution) Third Thursday: 8:00am-4:00pm, Following Friday: 8:00am until supplies last
    Who can get food

    Accessibility
    Follows ADA guideline of accessibility, drive through on west side of building, no matter your mode transportation. If you are walking or taking the bus, we recommend bringing something like a wagon or large reusable bags to help transport your groceries.

    Website · Directions

    More about this program

    The Lawton Food Bank is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that partners with the United Way of Southwest Oklahoma and the Regional Food Bank of Oklahoma to provide food assistance to low-income residents of southwest Oklahoma. Additionally, they maintain 11 retail partnerships to serve their clients. The Lawton Food Bank provides food and other items to low-income residents of southwest Oklahoma. In 2023, the Lawton Food Bank gave 1,262,723 pounds of food to 38,579 people. We also provided 216 boxes to prequalified seniors each month.
