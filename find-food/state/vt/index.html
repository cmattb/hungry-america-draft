    ​Feeding Champlain Valley, formerly known as Feeding Chittenden, partners with over 30 nonprofits and social service organizations to alleviate hunger in the Champlain Valley region. These collaborations include agricultural organizations, healthcare providers, housing organizations, and schools, all working together to improve health, maintain dignity, and challenge inequality. Food Bank serves Addison, Chittenden, Franklin, and Grand Isle counties. Services include education and training, grocery distribuiton, meal production, food rescue, deliveries to homes, and food access sites, an online market, food kiosks, and a food truck.

  7. Our Place Drop-In Center

    Soup Kitchen and Food Pantry

    Address
    4 Island St, Bellows Falls, VT, 05101
    Phone
    802-463-2217
    Hours
    Food Pantry Mon,Wed, Fri 9:00am-11:00am and 1:00pm-3:00pm, Breakfast served Mon-Fri 9:30am-10:30am, Lunch served Mon-Fri 11:30-12:30pm.
    Who can get food
    Food Pantry is open by appointment. We offer free breakfast and lunch to anyone who comes in during those hours, open to anyone. Our food pantry is open to any unhoused individuals and anyone with a permanent address in our catchment area.
    Accessibility
    Our Place also delivers about 40 bags of groceries each month to seniors and disabled people from Rockingham to Athens and south to Westminster. While this delivery program is currently of an informal and ad hoc nature with limited scope, it is an area of growth and outreach we are trying to ramp up and feature for our community food program.

    Website · Directions

    More about this program

    Our Place Drop-In Center has been serving the community surrounding Bellows Falls, Vermont for 25 years through homemade meals and grocery distribution. Our Place also helps direct people to other social services available such as applying for SNAP (food stamps), WIC, Commodity Supplemental Food Program, and Medicaid.

    Facebook: @OurPlaceDrop-InCenter
