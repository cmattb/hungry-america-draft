  2. Denmark Community Cupboard

    Food Pantry

    Address
    Denmark Veterans War Memorial Park Shelter House, Denmark, WI 54208
    Phone
    920-786-0525
    Hours
    Go to website to sign up for grocery delivery. Registration is annual. The 12 month cycle runs from September through August. Recipients can register at any time during the 12-month cycle, and will begin receiving groceries with the upcoming second Tuesday of the month. Recipients can register online on our webpage, or by calling us at (920) 786-0525.
    Who can get food
    Each recipient will receive groceries for a $10 suggested donation.A donation statement will be delivered with the groceries. Recipients may donate by check, cash, or online with a debit card. No one is denied groceries based on their ability to donate. The actual cost of the groceries is $100-150.
    Accessibility
    Follows ADA guideline of accessibility. We bring groceries to you.

    Website · Directions

    More about this program

    Our mission is to provide nutritious food to Denmark families who may need a helping hand. We promote generosity and gardening.
