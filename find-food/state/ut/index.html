    The Southeastern Utah Association of Local Governments (SEUALG) operates food banks in Carbon, Emery, and Grand counties, collaborating with various community organizations to address food insecurity in the region. Notable partnerships include:​ Utah Food Bank: SEUALG partners with the Utah Food Bank to increase food deliveries, enhancing the availability of food resources in Southeastern Utah. ​ Community Services Block Grant (CSBG) Program: Through discretionary funding from the CSBG, SEUALG supports initiatives aimed at reducing food insecurity among low-income populations. ​ Utahns Against Hunger: This collaboration focuses on increasing food deliveries to San Juan County and providing home deliveries to vulnerable populations, ensuring broader access to nutritious food. ​
