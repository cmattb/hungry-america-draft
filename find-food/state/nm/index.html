    More about this program

    ​Roadrunner Food Bank collaborates with a network of community partners to address hunger in New Mexico. According to their 2022-2023 Annual Report, they have 138 traditional partner agencies, which include food pantries, soup kitchens, group homes, and shelters. Additionally, they work with 336 partners across 16 counties served directly by their Albuquerque distribution center. This extensive network enables Roadrunner Food Bank to distribute millions of meals annually to individuals and families in need.​ Roadrunner Food Bank of New Mexico aims to alleviate hunger by distributing food to those in need through a network of community partners. They focus on ending food insecurity in New Mexico, providing support, education, and resources to ensure that no one goes hungry.
