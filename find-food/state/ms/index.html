    No eligibility requirements

  11. Stewpot Community Services

    Food Pantry

    Address
    1100 W Capitol St, Jackson, MS, 39203
    Phone
    601-353-2759
    Hours
    Mon-Fri 8:00am-4:00pm
    Who can get food
    Must be a resident of Hinds County. Please bring ID and proof of residence (a utility bill / copy of a lease) upon arrival, although no one is turned away. Please schedule an appointment by calling 601-353-2759 or emial myoung@stewpot.org to schedule (emergency cases will still be seen and assisted).
    Accessibility
    Follows ADA guidelines of accessibility. Travel bags are given to people without the ability to cook or refrigerate items.

    Website · Directions

    More about this program

    Stewpot Community Services provides compassionate assistance, at no charge, to people in need of physical and spiritual nourishment. Through our ministries, we serve people who are hungry, poor, and homeless, offering food, shelter, clothing, mentoring, care, and concern. At Stewpot, we believe that we all are fed through our fellowship with one another and that we are all stronger when offering our gifts on behalf of others.
