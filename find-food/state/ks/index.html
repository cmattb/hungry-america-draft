    Food Pantry

    Address
    1000 E. 11th St., Lawrence, KS 66046
    Phone
    785-856-7030
    Hours
    Tues and Thurs: 9:00am to 6:00pm, Wed and Fri: 9:00am to 3:00pm
    Who can get food
    All shoppers will be asked to verify household size. This is a one time verification. Acceptable forms of verification include (but aren't limited to): Driver's License, State ID, birth certificate, custody order, school enrollment verification, medical record, Foster care paperwork, etc. A completed and signed proxy authoriztion form allowing another person to shop for you (if acclicable)
    Accessibility
    ADA compliant. Clients who qualify for deliver: 1. Persons with a disability, chronic illness or health condition that keeps you from shopping in person 2. Persons who are homebound 3.     Website · Directions

    More about this program

    Just Food is a food bank in Douglas County, Kansas, supplying fresh and pantry staples to over 30 partner agencies. They operate a food pantry serving 300-600 families daily, addressing root causes of hunger, promoting self-sufficiency, and collaborating with community partners to ensure equitable access to nutritious food.
