    Phone

    Who can get food

    Accessibility
    At the Jane B. Gearhart Full Circle Food Pantry, there are doors propped open, wide doorways, signage and written materials in many different languages, food delivery and lockers (ADA accessible), located near public transportation, and volunteers available to help reach, lift and carry items.

    Website · Directions

    More about this program

    The Jane B. Gearhart Full Circle food pantry is a student-led emergency food assistance program, striving to serve clients with balanced meals through non-perishables and fresh produce from their own garden. They have canned proteins, beans, vegetables, fruit, pasta, rice, oatmeal, snack bars, soups, etc. They also have personal hygiene items like shower products, menstrual supplies, etc. They have fresh produce available from our garden including cherry tomatoes, kale, squash, banana peppers, egg plant, etc. They also have a food recovery resource program that operates out of the pantry called Razorback Food Recovery. This programs provides the pantry with varied fresh product.
