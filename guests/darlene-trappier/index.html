About Beacon of Hope

Pastor Darlene Trappier founded Beacon of Hope Inc., a faith-based nonprofit in Mount Holly, New Jersey, and leads it as executive director [UNVERIFIED: her preferred title]. Beacon of Hope began by giving out food and clothes, and it now provides weekly and emergency food, home food delivery, a clothes closet, a summer meal program for children, and help with security deposits, rent arrears, utility bills, and emergency motel placement. In winter it provides Code Blue sheltering as a contracted agency within the Burlington County homelessness system.
